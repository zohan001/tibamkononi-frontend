# Tibamkononi AI Features

## Overview

Tibamkononi integrates Gemma AI to assist healthcare professionals. The
AI supports clinical decision-making and hospital operations while
keeping clinicians in control of final decisions.

## AI Capabilities

### AI Triage

-   Analyzes patient symptoms
-   Suggests likely conditions
-   Recommends urgency level
-   Assists patient prioritization

### Clinical Decision Support

-   Suggests possible diagnoses
-   Highlights differential diagnoses
-   Recommends laboratory investigations
-   Suggests follow-up actions

### Medication Support

-   Recommends treatment options
-   Reviews prescription completeness
-   Warns about possible medication concerns
-   Encourages adherence to clinical guidelines

### Hospital Operations

-   Daily operational summaries
-   Bed occupancy insights
-   Staff attendance summaries
-   Inventory monitoring
-   Critical stock alerts

### Emergency Response

-   Helps prioritize emergency cases
-   Supports rapid hospital selection
-   Assists emergency dashboards

## Human Oversight

The AI is designed as a decision-support tool. Licensed healthcare
professionals remain responsible for diagnosis, treatment, and patient
care.

## Privacy

-   Patient information should be protected.
-   AI requests should follow applicable privacy regulations.
-   Sensitive information should be handled securely.

## Future Enhancements

-   Medical imaging assistance
-   Predictive disease analytics
-   County-wide outbreak monitoring
-   Voice-assisted clinical documentation
-   Personalized patient follow-up recommendations
