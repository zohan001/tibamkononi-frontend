# Tibamkononi -- Judges Evaluation Guide

## Project

**Project Name:** Tibamkononi

**Category:** Healthcare Innovation

## Executive Summary

Tibamkononi is an AI-powered healthcare management platform that
connects hospitals into one digital ecosystem. It combines electronic
medical records, AI-assisted triage, emergency response, inventory
management, appointments, staff management, and analytics.

## Problem

-   Long patient waiting times
-   Paper-based records
-   Poor emergency coordination
-   Medicine stock shortages
-   Limited hospital analytics

## Solution

-   Electronic Medical Records
-   AI-assisted triage
-   AI clinical insights
-   Inventory management
-   Bed management
-   Emergency coordination
-   Staff management
-   Reports and dashboards

## Demonstration Flow

1.  Landing page
2.  Hospital registration
3.  Dashboard
4.  Patient registration
5.  AI triage
6.  Diagnosis support
7.  Prescription
8.  Inventory
9.  Beds
10. Staff
11. Emergency
12. Reports

## AI Features

Gemma AI supports symptom analysis, clinical summaries, treatment
suggestions, inventory insights, and operational summaries. Healthcare
professionals remain responsible for final decisions.

## Scalability

Supports single hospitals, multiple hospitals, county-wide deployment,
and future national expansion.

## Conclusion

Tibamkononi modernizes healthcare delivery through integrated digital
workflows and AI-assisted decision support.
