# Tibamkononi Roadmap

## Vision

Tibamkononi aims to become the leading AI-powered healthcare management platform in Kenya and eventually across East Africa by digitizing healthcare operations and supporting medical professionals with artificial intelligence.

---

## Hospital Management

- Hospital registration
- Multi-hospital support
- Department management
- Ward management
- Bed management

## Patient Management

- Electronic Medical Records (EMR)
- Patient history

- Prescription records
