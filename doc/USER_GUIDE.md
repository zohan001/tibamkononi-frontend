# Tibamkononi User Guide

## Introduction

This guide helps hospital staff use Tibamkononi for daily healthcare
operations.

## Login

1.  Open the application.
2.  Enter your credentials.
3.  Sign in.

## Dashboard

The dashboard provides: - Patient statistics - Bed availability -
Inventory alerts - Staff attendance - AI operational insights

## Registering a Patient

1.  Open Patients.
2.  Click **Register Patient**.
3.  Complete the patient information.
4.  Save the record.

## Patient Records

-   Search patients
-   View medical history
-   Review diagnoses
-   Manage prescriptions

## Appointments

-   Schedule appointments
-   Update appointment times
-   View upcoming visits

## Inventory

-   Monitor stock
-   Record restocking
-   Track low-stock alerts
-   Review expiry dates

## Beds & Wards

-   View available beds
-   Monitor occupancy
-   Assign patients

## Staff

-   View attendance
-   Monitor staffing status

## Reports

Generate: - Daily reports - Monthly summaries - Inventory reports

## Emergency Module

-   Submit emergency requests
-   Monitor active emergencies
-   View emergency dashboard

## AI Features

Gemma AI assists with: - Clinical insights - Triage support - Medication
guidance - Operational summaries

Healthcare professionals remain responsible for all clinical decisions.

## Logging Out

Always log out after completing your work, especially on shared
computers.
